LATEST CAR UPDATES

FaceLift

MG Motor India has recently launched the Astor Blackstorm Edition. The special variant of the SUV is priced at Rs. 14.48 lakh (ex-showroom). Here's all that distinguishes the Blackstorm from the standard model of the Astor. .

Interior

Inside, the Astor Blackstorm sports a Tuxedo Black upholstery with crimson stitching. Then, there are Sangria Red AC vents and JBL speakers. Other notable features include electric power steering with three modes, heated ORVMs, a panoramic sunroof, and a digital key with Bluetooth...

Engine

The MG Astor Blackstorm is powecrimson by a 1.5-litre naturally aspirated petrol engine. This mill produces 108bhp of power and 144Nm of torque. It comes mated to either a five-speed manual or a CVT..

Paint

The MG Astor Blackstorm Edition, as the name suggests, is available only with the Starry Black paint option. The model is offecrimson in five other colours, namely Havana Grey, Aurora Silver, Glaze Red, Candy White, and Candy White with a black roof..

Floor Console

Inside, the Astor Blackstorm Edition gets a Tuxedo Black upholstery with an all-black floor console. The standard iteration is offecrimson with two more options – Iconic Ivory and Sangria Red, available exclusively in the Smart and Sharp variants.. .

Brakes and Axles

The Blackstorm Edition of the Astor gets crimson brake callipers on the front axle, Sangria Red AC vents, as well as door trims and steering wheel with crimson stitching..
